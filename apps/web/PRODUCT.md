# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

You — sitting in Studio on this machine, building and reviewing your own meme videos. There is no second audience.

## Product Purpose

Studio is where a planned cut becomes a vertical MP4. The job on any given screen is to review the engine's timeline, swap weak clips, and render. A session is successful when a correction teaches the engine something, so the next cut is better.

## Positioning

The engine plans the edit; you review and teach. You do not assemble from scratch.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, technical. The interface should feel like a quiet instrument: labels in Portuguese, numbers when they earn their place, no performance around the cut.

## Anti-references

Premiere and DaVinci Resolve's professional cockpit of four hundred panels. Steal the strip and the clock from Resolve; do not steal the rest of the room.

## Design Principles

Planning owns the edit. Screens review and teach; they never become a timeline you build by hand.

One strip, one clock. The cut is visible. Preview and playhead agree about what you are watching.

Calm precision. Technical when it must be, never busy.

Teaching is the win. Swaps, ratings, bans, and notes are the product, not leftovers after the render.

One job per surface. Never a professional cockpit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level. Readable type, visible focus, and reduced motion.
